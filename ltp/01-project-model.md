# 2R Research Circle project model

## Candidate goals

1. **Selected — G-1:** 2R inquiry becomes cumulative, plural, and
   action-guiding through a continually improving shared model.
2. **Improve the 2R LTP trees.** Directly supported, but too easy to satisfy
   through change without improvement.
3. **Enable better collective intelligence to solve the metacrisis.** True to
   the broader motivation, but too far outside the Research Circle's control.

## System boundary

Inside the Research Circle's influence:

- contributions from meetings, papers, presentations, and conversations;
- mapping contributions to existing claims or proposed new branches;
- evidence and provenance;
- standing, review, objection, disposition, and revision;
- research prioritization from model gaps;
- experiments in AI assistance and tree grafting.

Outside direct control:

- 2R-wide cultural adoption;
- civilization-level outcomes;
- individual development beyond research participation;
- other groups' goals and governance;
- institutional adoption of 2R practices.

## Throughput

Provisional unit:

> A reviewed, provenance-preserving improvement adopted into the shared 2R LTP
> model because of a research contribution.

Activity that is not itself throughput:

- meetings held;
- papers written;
- presentations delivered;
- AI matches proposed;
- tree edits made;
- proposals reviewed.

Rejected and disputed proposals can produce valuable learning and flow
information, but only adopted improvements count as throughput. "Improvement"
must ultimately include epistemic quality and downstream relevance, not merely
change volume.

## Actors

- Research contributors
- Conversation participants
- Model stewards/mappers
- Reviewers and objectors
- People or constituencies affected by 2R guidance
- 2R decision-makers and practitioners
- Peer organizations with their own trees
- AI mapping and synthesis assistants

## Main workflow

```text
natural contribution
→ source-preserving capture
→ candidate claim/model delta
→ contributor confirmation
→ governed review and objections
→ accepted / disputed / rejected / deferred disposition
→ versioned model update
→ dependency and research-priority update
→ later evidence about downstream use
```

## Current constraint

**RC-1:** No legitimate low-friction uptake loop connects research
contributions to versioned shared-model changes.

Governance, representation, interface, and matching problems are components of
that missing loop. Idea generation is not yet evidenced as the constraint.

## Core conflicts

1. Natural conversational freedom versus direct work in formal trees.
2. Local tree autonomy versus cross-group coherence through a universal tree.

## Direction of solution

Use a mediated dual-channel process: people speak and write naturally; stewards
and AI propose source-linked deltas; contributors confirm representation;
authorized reviewers dispose of proposals transparently. Later, use federated
grafts that preserve provenance, local branches, and disagreement rather than
predefining one everyone-level tree.

## Important contradictions

- The early platform assumes a single authoritative tree; the later vision
  rejects 2R's group tree as inherently universal.
- 2R proper and the Research Circle require different throughput definitions.
- AI matching is imagined before the target model and human review loop are
  mature.

## Authority

`ltp/ltp-model.yaml` is authoritative for identifiers, evidence, links, views,
the current constraint, and the next action.
