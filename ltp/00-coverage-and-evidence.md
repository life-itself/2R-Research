## Evidence status

- Participant statements and explicit project history are **observed** as
  source claims.
- The Research Circle goal is **confirmed** by the prompt and the user's request
  to make this repo conform to it.
- Causal explanations and expected effects remain **inferred** or
  **provisional**.
- The multi-scale grafting system is an exciting hypothesis, not an observed
  capability.

## Exclusions and gaps

No actual meeting artifacts, research papers, accepted revisions, decision
logs, contributor records, or external group trees were supplied. Therefore
the analysis cannot establish current conversion rates, review latency, model
quality, or grafting feasibility. No `throughput.yaml` was created.
