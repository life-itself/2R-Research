# Assumptions, questions, and contradictions

## Assumptions

| ID | Statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ASM-1 | David and Rufus are, for the time being, the same system as the 2R Research Circle. | confirmed |
| ASM-2 | If the two people who built the method do not use it consistently, the rest of the movement will not adopt it. | inferred |
| ASM-3 | Adoption requires that people can benefit from LTP outputs without doing tree work themselves. | confirmed |
| ASM-4 | Written, tracked commitments are what turn a tree into consistent practice. | provisional |
| ASM-5 | A tree cannot function as a source of truth without one canonical location and format. | inferred |
| ASM-6 | Evidence that LTP helps can only accumulate if the trees are actually used to assess progress. | inferred |
| ASM-7 | Presentation, rather than the logic itself, is what currently stops people getting value from a tree. | provisional |
| ASM-8 | The 2R goal tree is consequential enough that it must not be revised unilaterally. | confirmed |
| ASM-9 | Throughput for this system is new users of LTP and its outputs, not papers, meetings, or tree edits. | confirmed |

The two `provisional` assumptions carry the most weight for the recommendation:

- **`ASM-4`** is what makes `IC-4` the constraint. If tracked commitments are not
  what produce consistent practice, the whole transition tree is aimed at the
  wrong cause. This is the single assumption most worth testing, and `ACT-1`
  plus `ACT-2` together are a cheap test of it.
- **`ASM-7`** is David's own footnote — that salience and presentation sit behind
  several other problems. It supports `NC-15` and `IC-13`, neither of which is
  addressed by any current transition.

## Open questions

1. Is "we don't have a convincing story of how or where LTP would help" a cause
   of low buy-in, or an undesirable effect in its own right? The notes flag it
   both ways (`IC-3` / `NC-9`).
2. Which is the throughput unit for this system: new users of LTP and its
   outputs, as the whiteboard says, or duly improved 2R LTP trees, as David and
   Rufus agreed in July?
3. What does the "Learning" box on the whiteboard mean, and is it distinct from
   the two Results boxes? `LEARN-1` is currently an expansion of one word.
4. Is "discussion on the 2R forum is more productive and cumulative" the benefit
   to aim at? David wrote it with a question mark (`CSF-3`, `provisional`).
5. Who has standing to update the 2R goal tree, and by what process? Raised on
   the forum in June, still unanswered, and blocking `ACT-4`.
6. What counts as a new user of LTP or its outputs, over what period, and how
   would it be observed? Without this, the stated throughput cannot be measured.
7. How could linking forum or WhatsApp posts to tree nodes be automated, and is
   automation worth it before the manual path has been measured?
8. What are the intermediate objectives under the notes' "Conflict Resolution"
   heading, which is still an empty stub?
9. How much funding is needed, for what specifically, and from whom? `CSF-9` has
   nothing beneath it except `NC-11`.
10. The whiteboard states four success factors flat; is nesting them under
    "reasons for adoption" (`CSF-6`) right, or are they independent conditions?

## Contradictions

1. The whiteboard defines throughput as new users of LTP and its outputs, while
   the July exchange with Rufus agreed the research group's throughput is duly
   improved 2R LTP trees. Different units, different priorities. Recorded, not
   merged.
2. Having no evidence that LTP is helping is given as a cause of low buy-in, but
   the trees are also not being used to assess progress, so that evidence cannot
   accumulate. The two reinforce each other and neither is prior.
3. Adoption is wanted movement-wide, yet the format, tracker, and viewer needed
   to sustain it do not exist even for the two people who built the method.
4. Discoverability work such as the Bluesky bridge was attempted while the trees
   were, by David's own account, not mature enough for the suggestions to be
   poignant.
5. Finalizing a presentable 2R goal tree is needed for forum integration, but
   doing so without an agreed update process would repeat the June problem of
   unilateral revision.
