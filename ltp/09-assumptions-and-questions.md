## Contradictions

1. Early platform thinking assumes one authoritative tree; the later insight
   rejects a pre-imposed everyone tree.
2. 2R-wide throughput measures durable cultural adoption; research throughput
   measures adopted shared-model improvement.
3. Natural conversation and tree discipline are both valued, but the bridge is
   untested.
4. AI matching is proposed while its target trees and matching quality are
   described as immature.

## Legitimate reservations

- **Clarity:** define contribution, delta, improvement, shared model, graft,
  everyone tree, standing, and adoption.
- **Entity existence:** verify that undisposed contributions and recurring
  discussions are current, material effects.
- **Causality existence:** test whether a missing uptake loop causes low model
  improvement.
- **Cause insufficiency:** research quality, participation, time, trust, or
  facilitation may also constrain.
- **Additional cause:** institutional authority outside the circle may block
  adoption.
- **Cause-effect reversal:** weak trees may cause weak uptake, not only result
  from it.
- **Predicted effect:** pre-register what a successful manual pilot should
  produce.
- **Tautology:** do not define improvement as any accepted change.
- **Boundary:** do not attribute 2R-wide cultural outcomes directly to the
  Research Circle.
