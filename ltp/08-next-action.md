# Recommended next action

## Action

**ACT-1 — Run one manual contribution-to-tree pilot using a recent Research
Circle paper or session.**

## Why it is next

It is the smallest reversible action that tests the central constraint and
several decisive assumptions at once:

- whether useful contributions already exist;
- whether contributors tolerate mediated mapping;
- whether a proposed model delta can preserve their meaning;
- whether provisional governance can produce a legitimate disposition;
- whether the result is plausibly an improvement rather than merely an edit;
- where a future interface or AI assistant would actually help.

Building a platform first would optimize an untested workflow. Debating a
permanent constitution first would lack operating evidence.

## Constraint addressed

RC-1 — the absent legitimate, low-friction uptake loop.

## Obstacle removed

OBS-4 — no end-to-end evidence that a real contribution can improve a real
tree.

## Expected immediate effect

TR-1 — one source-linked model delta receives an accepted, disputed, rejected,
or deferred disposition, with participant burden and process friction
recorded.

## Preconditions

- one recent contribution;
- one canonical tree version;
- contributor consent;
- mapper/steward and two reviewers;
- one-page provisional charter, governance rules, and delta template;
- a time-box and rollback rule.

## Completion evidence

- source and excerpt;
- target node/link IDs;
- proposed addition, revision, split, dispute, or removal;
- evidence, rationale, assumptions, and rival interpretation;
- contributor confirmation or correction;
- objections and reasoned disposition;
- resulting version or explicit non-change;
- elapsed effort and friction notes.

## Risks

- Pilot participants gain accidental permanent authority.
- Formality chills conversation.
- A change is counted as improvement without epistemic or practical value.
- The chosen contribution is unrepresentative.

Containment: provisional status, sunset, withdrawal, source preservation,
minority reports, one branch only, and an explicit coverage limit.

## What becomes possible

The group can revise governance from evidence, define a defensible throughput
record, evaluate AI against a human baseline, and specify a real user
interface. A failed pilot would reveal whether governance, representation, or
the model itself is the tighter constraint.

## Confidence

High that this is the highest-leverage next action; medium that a suitable
canonical tree and authorized participants are available now.

## Do not start yet

1. Do not build the Bluesky acquisition bridge; the uptake loop and target
   model are not validated.
2. Do not automate adoption decisions; AI has no evidenced authority or
   baseline accuracy.
3. Do not create an everyone-level master tree; first test one reversible
   pairwise graft.

## Evidence that would overturn this recommendation

- A documented contribution-to-tree process already operates reliably.
- The Research Circle cannot identify an authorized current tree or reviewers.
- Research quality or participation, rather than uptake, is shown to be the
  actual constraint.
